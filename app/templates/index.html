How to use:

  1. Download the appropriate answer sheet template.
  2. Print it and fill in your answers by marking the appropriate bubbles.
  3. Take a photo of the completed answer sheet (ensure good lighting and the entire sheet is visible).
  4. Select the correct sheet type (NAPOLCOM or Original).
  5. Upload the photo to this page.
  6. View the results showing which answers were detected.

Note for NAPOLCOM sheets: The system uses the signature box at the bottom for alignment, so make sure it's clearly visible in your photo.

Note for Original OMR sheets: The system looks for corner marks to align the sheet, so make sure all four corners are visible in your photo.
